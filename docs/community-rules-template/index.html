Copy-paste community rules, including no financial advice and wallet safety

Short answer: Good community rules are short, specific and enforceable: be respectful, no spam or unsolicited DMs, no financial advice or price promises, never share seed phrases or private keys, official links only come from admins, and a clear warning, mute, ban ladder.

Copy-paste rules

📌 COMMUNITY RULES

1. Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech or personal attacks.
2. No spam, shilling or unsolicited promotion. No unsolicited DMs to members.
3. No financial advice. Nothing here is investment advice. Do your own research.
   No price predictions presented as fact, no "guaranteed" returns.
4. Wallet safety: NEVER share your seed phrase or private key with anyone.
   No admin will ever ask for it.
5. Admins will NEVER DM you first. Anyone who does is a scammer. Report them.
6. Official links are only posted in #announcements by admins.
7. No impersonation of team members, projects or other users.
8. Stay on topic. Use the right channel.
9. English in main chat unless a channel says otherwise.
10. Moderators' decisions are final; appeal politely via [contact].

Enforcement: warning → mute (24h) → ban. Scams and impersonation = instant ban.

Adapting the rules

  • Replace channel names and the appeal contact.
  • Add a language rule if you run regional channels.
  • Keep the list short enough to read in one screen; link longer policies separately.

Why a no-financial-advice rule matters

It sets expectations that discussion is not advice, discourages pump-style posting and reduces the chance members act on hype. It does not replace any legal obligations your project has in its jurisdictions.

FAQ

What rules should a crypto community have?

At minimum: respect, no spam or unsolicited DMs, no financial advice, never share seed phrases or private keys, admins never DM first, official links only from admins, and a clear enforcement ladder.

How do I write a no financial advice rule?

State that nothing in the community is investment advice, ban guaranteed-return claims and price predictions presented as fact, and remind members to do their own research.

Should scams get an instant ban?

Yes. Most communities skip warnings for scams, phishing links and impersonation and ban immediately.
